# BeatNova Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 11, 2026**

BeatNova is a music application. This policy explains, at a high level, how the app handles information.

## Information and services

BeatNova may use Supabase for account authentication and application data. Information provided during account use may include an email address and authentication/session information needed to provide the service.

BeatNova uses Adivery for advertising. Advertising providers may process advertising-related identifiers and technical information according to their own policies and applicable law.

The app may also use device/network information that is technically necessary to load media, authenticate requests, display advertisements, deliver notifications, or operate downloads.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide account features, authenticate users, operate playback and downloads, maintain app functionality, display advertisements, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

## Data sharing

BeatNova does not sell users' personal information. Service providers such as Supabase and Adivery may process information on BeatNova's behalf to provide authentication, backend, advertising, and related services.

## Security

BeatNova uses HTTPS for network communication where supported by the service configuration. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention and deletion

Account and application data may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations. Users may request deletion of account-related data where applicable.

## Children's privacy

BeatNova is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the support contact published with the BeatNova Myket listing.
